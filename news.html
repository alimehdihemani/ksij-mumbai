22 November 2024

The WF President’s India trip, November 2024

The WF President, Alhaj Safder Jaffer, visited Mumbai from 1st to 3rd November 2024, engaging with community leaders, exploring key development projects and strengthening partnerships to support local initiatives. The visit included stops in surrounding areas of Mumbai, focusing on educational, housing and heritage projects critical to the welfare and growth of the community, and attended to spiritual developments within the community.

Meeting with Sayyid Ahmedali Abedi sahab. The visit began with a meeting with Sayyid Ahmedali Abedi sahab, Representative of His Eminence Sayyid al-Sistani (dz) in India, to discuss collaboration between Al Muntazar Academy and the Islamic Education sector of The World Federation, reviewing the university’s curriculum, infrastructure and potential for joint educational programmes.

Visit to Dongri. In the evening the WF team explored heritage sites on foot and by motorcycle through the narrow, bustling lanes, including the historic Mulla Qadar’s site and a boys’ school managed by the Jamaat. Discussions included enhancing these spaces and new initiatives such as a girls’ school in the vicinity.

Aarambaug (Khoja Kabrastan). With the Office Bearers of the Mumbai Jamaat, the President paid respect to community leaders at Arambaug Kabrastan and reviewed opportunities to enhance its facilities. He was impressed by how the Kabrastan has been upgraded, and in particular how the Khoja Wall has become a place of contemplation on how we have evolved as a faith-based community through the sacrifices of the prominent personalities buried there.

18 November 2023 · Kesar Baug Hall

Students Felicitation Function 2023

Held for the first time after the Covid pandemic, the function recognised students for 3 academic years, 2020 to 21, 2021 to 22 and 2022 to 23. Around 380+ students (boys and girls) were felicitated for completing SSC and HSC with above 70%, and Graduation and Post Graduation with 60% and above, including Chartered Accountants, lawyers, medical professionals, surgeons and other professional degrees.

The function was hosted by KSI Jamat Mumbai with Hon. President Mr. Ali Akbar Shroff, Vice President Mr. Mohammad Raza Parekh, the Office Bearers, Managing Committee and Education Committee. The Chief Guest was the President of the World Federation of KSIMC, Shaikh Safdar Jaffer.

Guests of honour (Gents): Al Haj Aqae Maulana Sayed Ahmed Ali Abedi (Vakil of Grand Ayatullah Sayed Ali Al Huseini Al Sistani dz); Mr. Amin Patel, MLA of Mumbadevi constituency; Maulana Sayyed Abid Bilgirami Sahab. (Ladies): Dr. Bharati Lavekar, MLA Versova; Ms Ranjana Patil, Municipal Corporator. Mrs Nafisa Ali Akbar Shroff presided over the ladies’ section.

The dignitaries spoke on the importance of education, and Shaikh Safder Jaffer and Mr. Ali Akbar Shroff pledged to financially support deserving students. Around 1,000+ guests attended, including parents, students and well wishers.

11 February 2024

Shaikh Mohammad Al Hilli lecture series

The distinguished Shia Muslim Aalim, Shaikh Mohammad Al Hilli, visited Mumbai for a series of lectures organised by KSI Jamat Mumbai across Bandra, Dongri, Kurla and Andheri, drawing both enthusiastic youth and seasoned elders.

The initiative created a platform for spiritual enlightenment and intellectual growth, reaching every corner of the city, from the bustling streets of Bandra to the historic lanes of Dongri, and fostering unity and shared learning. KSI Jamat Mumbai thanks all the attendees for making it a success.
