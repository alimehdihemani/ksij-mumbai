Continuous projects

Where your support goes.

Medical and hospitalisation

Continuous project

Medical care and hospitalisation are becoming expensive and unaffordable for the common man in Mumbai. The needy and underprivileged look to the Jamat to meet their monthly medical bills, investigations and hospitalisation.

  • Disbursed, FY 2021 to 22Rs. 75.37 lacs

Educational assistance

Continuous project

Every year, KSI Jamat, Mumbai financially supports needy community students in their academic pursuits.

  • FY 2018 to 19Rs. 62.38 lacs
  • FY 2019 to 20Rs. 57.72 lacs
  • FY 2021 to 22Rs. 69.55 lacs

Hajatmand

Helping widows and divorcees · Continuous project

During Mahe Ramadhan 1444 (March and April 2023) the Jamaat distributed the Mahe Ramadhan Food Basket, Sehri Basket, Eid Barakah Basket and financial gifts to everyone.

  • Widows supported every Ramadhan~220
  • Hajatmand families every Ramadhan~550

Bookings & membership

Book with the Jamaat.

Each request goes to the Hon. Joint Secretary. Please confirm any outstanding dues with the Manager of the Jamat first.

Patent Majlis

To: Hon. Joint Secretary
KSI Jamat, Mumbai

Dear Sir,
Salamun Alaikum

I would like to book a Patent Majlis for the following three Marhumeen and would like the Majlis and Fatiha to be recited on my chosen date. Fatiha for all three Marhumeen will be recited in one Majlis on the selected date.

Bookings can be set for 2 days ahead, as a request takes 48 hours to process.

Send this request

Qaumi Sahayak

KSI Jamat, Mumbai

Dear Sir,
Salamun Alaikum

I would like to pay for Qaumi Sahayak.
