Office bearers

The people who serve.

Hon. President

Mr. Ali Akbar M. Shroff

+91 81045 73301president@ksijamat.org

Aliakbar Shroff is a noted philanthropist and social worker from India. He has been involved with many charities and social projects in India over the last 15 years. He has a business of import and distribution of body care products and exports of stationery products, in addition to being a real estate investor. Aliakbar is married with 3 children, 2 of whom have joined him in his business and the third is studying.

Read full profile

Aliakbar is a Trustee with Kesarbaug Trust, Khoja Girls Orphanage and Haji Nazarali Imambargah amongst others. He has also served the KSI Jamaat Mumbai as Secretary for 3 years and as Vice President for 4.5 years. During his association with KSI Jamaat Mumbai he was instrumental in bringing focus to Education, Medical, Economic Upliftment and Capacity Building, all of which grew 3 to 4 times in a span of 7 to 8 years.

He was also instrumental in the formation of the India Federation (parent body of all KSI Jamaats of India) in 2013, uniting the diverse community across Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka and Telangana on one platform towards common developmental objectives and coordinated relief work. He served as the Secretary of the India Federation from inception until 2021 (8 years).

Aliakbar has also led notable capital projects, including 2 graveyard projects in Mumbai, the redevelopment of Haji Nazarali Imambargah into a modern facility with state of the art infrastructure, and the refurbishment of KesarBaug Hall, the KSI Jamaat office, Madressa and Arambaug in Mumbai. He is also the first elected Councillor to represent the India region at the World Federation, elected for 2 consecutive terms from 2017 to 2021 and from 2021 to 2024. Aliakbar was also the JIBA president for the Mumbai chapter and went on to serve as the President of JIBA International for one term.

Hon. Vice President

Mr. Mohamed Raza D. Parekh

+91 98211 99724secretariat@ksijamat.org

Haji Mohammed Raza Dost Mohammed Parekh is more popularly known as Haji bhai by everyone. Haji bhai is a known philanthropist from Mumbai with multiple business interests built over the last 3 to 4 decades. He served the Mumbai Jamat as Joint Secretary between 2018 and 2019, and is a Trustee with various other Trusts such as Kesar Baug Trust, which supports hundreds of students in higher education.

Read full profile

He was also appointed as Special Executive Officer by the Government of Maharashtra, a great achievement. He is an expert in Income Tax and GST related activities and serves numerous clients across the state.

Beyond his professional life he is a great community worker. He is a Trustee of the Islamic Volunteer Troop (IVT), which serves the Azadar e Imam Husein (a.s.) during Mahe Aza. He was a Consulting Committee member for more than a decade, served as Internal Auditor for KSI Jamat Mumbai on an honorary basis for several years, and was appointed to the Managing Committee in recent years, currently handling secretarial responsibilities.

Haji bhai is also a renowned reciter of Marsiya, Nauhas and Manqabats of the Ahlulbayt (a.s.), and one cannot miss his recitations in Mumbai, especially during Muharram, at various Imambargahs and Mosques.

Hon. Secretary

Mr. Sajjad Pyarali Shroff

Haji Sajjad P Shroff is the owner of Gujarat Melamine. Previously he was in the manufacturing business of melamine crockery and powder under the brand name Ornet Melamine. He comes from a family with a rich history in community service over many decades, and has himself served the Khoja community in his personal capacity and under various committees and Trusts for several years.

Read full profile

He is a trustee with the Khoja Shia Ithna Ashari Madrasa Imambada Trust and Jama-Masjid, where he also serves as Treasurer. He has been Secretary of the Madrasa Qirat Committee for nearly two decades, and has been associated with the Mahe Muharram Niyaz Committee and the Imam-e-Zamana (a.s.) Niyaz Committee for many years. Sajjad Shroff is also a trustee of the Khoja Student League and Khadijatul Kubra Trust.

Beyond his business he is well known for his reliable services as a medical consultant for Khoja families coming to India for medical treatment and emergencies.

Hon. Joint Secretary

Mr. Nadeem Parvez Halani

Nadeem Halani is a Science graduate from Mumbai and works as an Analyst and Service Manager at Deutsche Bank, with 15 years in the banking industry. He started community service in 2008, volunteering for medical and education aid for economically backward brethren, and was then appointed to handle medical and education cases for the Mumbai Jamaat.

Read full profile

He has facilitated education aid and assistance for many young students through the Mumbai Jamaat and other community charities. Nadeem Halani has a family of 6 and lives with his parents, wife and two young children.

Hon. Treasurer

Mr. Irfan Mohsin Karim

Irfan Karim is a qualified Chartered Accountant and Cost Accountant who has spent over 25 years in the corporate world in India, Singapore and Indonesia. His last role was CFO of an Indian research and data analytics company with a global footprint; after completing its sale to an American MNC he took a sabbatical, and has since worked as a strategy and finance consultant.

Read full profile

His community work began in 2007, when he and his friends supported parents with primary education for children in slum areas of the Mumbai suburbs, admitting many children into local schools. Over the last few years he has focused on supporting students from all over India in higher and professional education, in line with Syed Sistani’s guidance that our community youth should excel in professional fields.

He has been involved with community and developmental projects with the Mumbai Khoja Jamat, the India Federation and many other charities, and with numerous Imambargah capital projects, including the first Shia Imambargahs in Kerala and Goa. With senior ulema from India he has co-developed and co-delivered workshops on Islamic Business (Ethics and Principles), Khums, and Wills and Inheritance across many cities.

Hon. Joint Treasurer

Mr. Rizwan Fida Husain Batliwala

Rizwan Fidahussain Batliwala is a graduate from Mumbai and an entrepreneur who set up a perfume business; he is currently Managing Director of Alif Perfumes and Fragrances.

Read full profile

Rizwan bhai has volunteered for community service since a very young age. He started as a scout with the Mahdi Scout Troops and became Head Master of the Scout Group. He is an active member of Anjuman e Shamsheri Haidery, which organises programmes throughout the year to commemorate important Islamic dates, including Azadari during Muharram. He is also a trustee with Caravan e Hind Mokib at pole number 327 in Najaf, Iraq, and has been a Managing Committee member with the Mumbai Jamat for many years.

Legal advisor

Serving the Jamat since 1995.

Legal Advisor since 1995 · Senior Partner, Patel Associates

Advocate Sajjad H. Patel

+91 98201 03219advpatel@rediffmail.com

Sajjad H. Patel is a graduate of GLC Law College and obtained his degree in law in 1989 from the University of Mumbai; since then he has practised as an advocate registered with the Bar Council of Maharashtra and Goa. He worked as an associate advocate with Bilawala & Co Advocates & Solicitors for 4 years and then for 1 year under Bharat Joshi, Senior Advocate, before starting his individual practice in 1992 as Advocate S. H. Patel.

Read full profile

He has excelled in many fields of civil law, including property law, banking law, tenancy, negotiable instruments and the law of trusts, and after 32 years of practice has achieved the position of Senior Advocate in all courts of law. He encouraged his wife, Mrs. Neelam S. Patel, to become an advocate too, and she has since achieved a master’s in law. His office has grown into the firm PATEL ASSOCIATES, Advocates & Legal Consultants.

He has been the legal advisor of KSIJ since 1995, serving the Jamat on a voluntary basis, and also serves Habib Group of Trust, Mehfil e Kurashan, Shustary Imambada Trust, Anjuman-e-Fotowat Trust and Dawoodbhoy Fazalbhoy Trust, among others. He is empanelled with many nationalised banks, co-operative societies, developers and public charitable trusts.

He recently won a case pending since 1995 for KSI Jamat, which went up to the Bombay High Court, through which the Jamat regained possession of premises of approximately 2,000 sq. ft. for a school, held by the Mumbai Municipal Corporation for several years. At the age of 59, his dedication to his profession, KSI Jamat and other trusts is the same as on the day of his appointment.
